Autonomous Motion with Zero Latency

AncomeVortex builds advanced robotic motion platforms that interact dynamically with unpredictable environments. We combine direct torque control, harmonic drive gearboxes, and multi-axis spatial vision to deliver unmatched repeatable precision.

Whether deploying heavy-load warehouse AMRs or microscopic cleanroom assembly arms, our firmware executes on real-time micro-kernels with deterministic execution times under 200 microseconds.

  • Sub-Millimeter Repeatability: ±0.01 mm positional accuracy under dynamic loads.
  • Human-Safe Cobots: Haptic collision detection meeting ISO 10218-1 / ISO/TS 15066 safety mandates.
  • LiDAR SLAM Navigation: Real-time mapping and obstacle avoidance in changing floorplans.
